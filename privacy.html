fu2ureyou Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

I’m Kawser, and I care about your privacy, so here is the plain version.

What I collect: When you log in with TikTok, the app receives a login token and basic profile information (your username). That’s all it uses to upload videos you choose.

Where it’s kept: The token is saved only on my own computer. It isn’t sent to any server or website.

What I don’t do: I don’t sell your data, share it with anyone, show ads, or track you.

Deleting your data: You can remove the app’s access at any time in TikTok under Settings, then Security, then Manage app permissions. You can also email me and I’ll delete anything I have.

Questions: Email me at fu2ureyou@gmail.com.
